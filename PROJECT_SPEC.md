# PROJECT_SPEC.md — Upbhokta Setu

## Product Name
**Upbhokta Setu (उपभोक्ता सेतु)** — "Your consumer grievance, connected."

## Problem
Indian consumers filing grievances with the National Consumer Helpline, consumer forums, or e-Jagriti often lack organized evidence, a clear statement of their transaction relationship, and knowledge of the applicable legal provisions. The process is paper-heavy, confusing, and many valid complaints are dismissed on technical grounds.

## Solution
A bilingual mobile-first platform that helps consumers:
1. Record their transaction and evidence in an organized form.
2. Get AI-assisted explanation of their rights and the relevant legal provisions.
3. Prepare a structured grievance draft with citations.
4. Track case status once submitted.

## Target Users
- Indian consumers filing grievances (primary).
- Consumer activists / NGOs helping others (secondary, future).

## MVP Scope
| In scope | Out of scope (later phases) |
|----------|------------------------------|
| Create and track a single grievance case | Multi-case management dashboard |
| Evidence upload (photos, documents) | Bulk import from other platforms |
| AI-assisted rights explanation | Automated filing with NCH/e-Jagriti |
| Bilingual (English / हिन्दी) | Regional language expansion |
| Offline draft preparation | E-signature integration |

## Success Criteria
- A consumer can create a case, upload evidence, and get a bilingual AI explanation of their rights within 10 minutes on a 4G connection.
- Language switching is immediate and persistent.
- AI-generated content is clearly labeled and not presented as legal advice.
